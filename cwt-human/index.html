Exhibit tips & controls

Use one participant, even lighting, and a clear view of the camera. A recent Chrome or Edge browser is a good starting point on Windows or macOS.

Inside the demo, Models switches features on and off. Display changes overlays. Options includes performance settings. The first inference can take a little longer while models download.

Pause video pauses analysis but keeps the camera connected. Use Stop & reset above to release it. If access is denied, allow the camera in your browser’s site settings and relaunch. Internet access is required to load the library and models.

About this demo & attribution

Presented by the Construction Workforce & Technology Lab. The interface wraps a locally hosted adaptation of Human by Vladimir Mandic, with the original model and display controls.

Human is used under the MIT License. See credits and source details for the upstream version, model attribution, and CWT adaptations.

Camera frames are processed in your browser. This exhibit does not record or upload them. Expression categories are model estimates, not measurements of a person’s internal emotional state.
